Tests of sphinx-localtime:

localtime¶

The localtime role:

  • 13 Aug 2024 10:00:00 +03:00: .

  • 13 Aug 2024 10:00:00 +03:00: .

  • 13 Aug 2024 10:00:00 +03:00 (D MMM HH:mm):

  • 13 Aug 2024 10:00:00 +03:00 (HH:mm z):

  • 13 Aug 2024 10:00:00 +03:00 (HH:mm zzz):

localtime2¶

The localtime2 role has a different hover text and is designed for showing the timezone without a date. The date (and maybe time) is important since it gets the detected timezone at that date/time.

  • 13 Aug 2024 (zzz): Your detected timezone is

  • 13 Dec 2024 (zzz): Your detected timezone is

  • 13 Aug 2024 (z): Your detected timezone is

  • 13 Dec 2024 (z): Your detected timezone is
